## About The Project

**KWhane** (*kWh + hane [household]*) is a 3D digital-twin home energy simulation and intelligent savings platform specifically designed for Turkish households.

Traditional electricity bills only present a single lump-sum figure without revealing which specific appliances consume the most power. **KWhane** solves this by letting users construct an interactive **3D digital twin** of their home, place household appliances via drag-and-drop, and instantly receive device-level kWh predictions, cost diagnostics, and AI-driven energy optimization strategies.

---

## Key Features

### 1. 3D Home Digital Twin & Real-Time Simulation
- Built using **React Three Fiber (R3F)** and Three.js.
- Interactive drag-and-drop placement of rooms, walls, lighting, and household appliances.
- Floating real-time **energy badges** displayed above each appliance showing estimated monthly kWh and TL cost.

### 2. Physics-Informed Machine Learning Engine
- Powered by a custom **GradientBoostingRegressor** (scikit-learn) trained on physics-informed synthetic datasets (5,000+ samples).
- Predicts monthly kWh consumption taking into account:
  - Nominal wattage & operational duty cycles
  - Energy efficiency classes (**A+++** through **G**)
  - Device age degradation (**+1.5% consumption per year**)
  - **Turkey's Tiered Electricity Tariff** system (~1.50 ₺/kWh low tier vs ~2.30 ₺/kWh high tier).

### 3. Peer Clustering & Benchmarking
- Implements **K-Means Clustering** ($k=5$) to categorize households into consumption tiers.
- Allows users to benchmark their monthly energy consumption against similar household setups in their region.

### 4. Bilingual Conversational AI Energy Advisor
- Integrated Turkish & English conversational assistant powered by **Llama 3.3 70B** (via Groq API) / **Llama 3.2** (via local Ollama).
- Context-aware RAG pipeline: The AI accesses live household data (installed devices, calculated monthly costs, bill history, and active recommendations) to deliver tailored advice.

### 5. Smart Savings & Rule-Based Scoring Engine
- Automated recommendation engine that flags high-energy appliances.
- Generates actionable savings tips (e.g., standby power reduction via smart plugs, peak-load shifting, appliance upgrade ROI calculations).

### 6. Bill Calibration & Diagnostics
- Import real electricity utility bills to calibrate ML model predictions against actual household consumption.
- Tracks prediction accuracy over time for continuous model fine-tuning.
